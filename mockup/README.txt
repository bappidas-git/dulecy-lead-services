Dulecy Lead Services — Static Website

Files:

Notes:
- GSAP + ScrollTrigger and Google Fonts load from CDNs (internet required).
- The lead form shows a success message only; connect it to your email/CRM backend
  (e.g. Formspree, Google Apps Script, or your server) inside scripts.js.
- Open index.html in any browser, or upload all files to any static host.
